Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • FYN Enterprises

At FYN (https://fynstore.in), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our store or purchase travel gear.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, create an account, or join the travel community, we collect: Name, email address, WhatsApp/mobile number, delivery address, city, and pincode.

2. Payment Information

All online payments (NetBanking, UPI, and Cards) are processed through RBI-authorized payment aggregators using bank-grade 256-bit encryption. FYN never stores your raw bank passwords or credit card CVV numbers.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared strictly with logistics partners (e.g., Blue Dart, Delhivery) for physical package delivery and tracking SMS/emails.
